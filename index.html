Gel pseudo-chromatograms

Biotin-GPP in vitro prenylation of mouse blood lysates, streptavidin blots. Every lane read as a trace on one kDa axis from its gel's ladder.

Lanes

Height = a fraction of a saturated pixel on the same image, above the lane's local background (1 = saturated; a flat top means saturated). Compare heights within one gel; between gels, sizes hold to about 5–10%. Drag to zoom, double-click to reset.

Gel images

Click a band on the gel to pick it: it's marked on every lane (✓ seen, ✗ not seen, ≈ covered by a neighbour, ? faint), on the plot and in the band table. Switch to "Click picks a lane" to add or remove lanes instead. Shaded columns are the pixels averaged into each trace; orange ticks are the ladder bands as found. Hovering over the plot marks that size on the gel.

Notebook notes for this gel

Each selected lane cut from its gel beside that gel's ladder, every strip stretched onto the same kDa scale so sizes line up across gels. Contrast is each image's own. Click a strip to pick the band there; with a band picked, the mark under each strip says whether it's seen (✓), not seen (✗), covered by a neighbour (≈) or faint (?). Hovering over the plot marks that size across the strips.

Bands

Click a row to highlight that band on the plot and the gels (click again to clear); clicking a band on the plot or a gel picks it too. "Show evidence" loads the lanes behind the claim onto the plot and the side-by-side strips, and lists every lane where the band is seen and where it isn't.
